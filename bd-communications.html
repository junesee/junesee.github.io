Internal Communications

From one newsletter to a year-round programme.
Bosch Digital Singapore · Internal Comms · Content · Events · Visual Design
Overview
When our marketing colleague left Bosch Digital Singapore in 2022, there was no one left to handle communications. I took it over alongside my UX work, starting with a newsletter in October 2022.

Until then, comms happened as and when needed, with no overall plan.
The challenge wasn't writing one more email. It was making communication consistent.
My Role
Internal communications, alongside my role as Senior UX Designer, from October 2022 to June 2026. Audience: about 100 associates in Singapore, plus regional colleagues.
What I Did
Planned the year, not just the next email.
In 2024, I created Bosch Digital Singapore's first annual communication plan, mapping out newsletters, events and key moments in advance, so comms became consistent instead of reactive.

Made wins visible.
I started BD Success Stories to share project highlights, so teams could see what others were achieving.

Brought people together.
I worked with the events team on monthly engagement activities: townhalls, Show & Tell sessions, CSR activities, team hikes and year-end celebrations. My part was the messaging and visuals, the invitations, announcements and posters that got people to show up.

Connected beyond Singapore.
I ran Comm Connect sessions with the Vietnam team and created content for the Bosch ASEAN and Bosch Digital LinkedIn pages.
What I Made
  • Annual communication plan
  • Quarterly newsletters, written and designed
  • BD Success Stories
  • Invitations, announcements and posters for monthly events, created in Canva
  • LinkedIn posts for company pages with 196k+ followers combined
Outcome
Oct 2022 Took over comms and sent the first newsletter
2024 Introduced the team's first annual communication plan
Jun 2026 A year-round programme of newsletters, success stories, monthly events and LinkedIn content

Kept it running for almost four years without a dedicated comms role.
What This Demonstrates
  • Internal communications planning
  • Writing and designing employee content
  • Supporting engagement through events
  • Stepping up when a role is left empty

💭 Reflection
‍ Good comms works like good UX.
When it's done well, nobody notices it.
People just know what's going on.
