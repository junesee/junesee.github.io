Human-Centered UX Designer & Researcher
👋 Hi, I‘m Joanne.
My career has never followed a straight line.
Growing up, I wanted to study Mass Communications because I was fascinated by storytelling, and how ideas influence people. Instead, I pursued a degree in Urban Planning.
Looking back, it taught me how to think about systems, communities, and the way people interact with the environments around them.
After graduation, I worked across cartography, UX design, user research, and communications. None of these were part of some master plan but opportunities that appeared as I followed my curiosity.
Despite the different job titles, there has always been a common thread:
🧩 How do people make sense of the world around them?
Whether I'm designing experiences, conducting research, communicating ideas, or exploring emerging technologies, I'm driven by a desire to better understand people and help make complex things a little easier to navigate.
